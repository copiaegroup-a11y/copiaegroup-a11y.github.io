About

Mary Ellen Lavoie

Developmental editor, writer, and consultant with Copiae Group since 2020.

Through Copiae Group I work with clients on a contract basis: coaching authors through book-length manuscripts and into publication, and helping early-stage startups define their Scope of Work, Minimum Viable Product, and pitch materials. All of my client work is remote, over Zoom, phone, and shared documents.

Every engagement follows the same approach: understand where the client is starting from, define a clear structure, and work alongside them until the project is complete.

How I work

Start where you are

Whether it is a partial draft, a first lesson, or a broad business idea, I begin by understanding what you already have and what you need.

Build the structure

Outlines, phases, and clear deliverables turn a large goal into manageable steps that both of us can track.

Stay with it to the end

I work alongside clients through every revision, so the finished work is clear, consistent, and ready to use.
