Developmental editing and writing coaching

Developmental editing goes beyond proofreading. It addresses the shape and substance of a manuscript.

  • Developing the topic and planning the overall structure of the book
  • Building a working outline
  • Chapter-by-chapter coaching through multiple rounds of revision
  • Reorganizing the manuscript: moving content between chapters, closing gaps, and identifying where new material is needed
  • Establishing a consistent voice and tone
  • Guidance on cover concept as a marketing tool
  • Preparing and publishing the manuscript on Amazon's platform in all available formats
